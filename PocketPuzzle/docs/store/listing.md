## Full description

Little puzzles, big smiles. Meet friendly parcel and block pals in PocketPuzzle,
a portrait puzzle collection you can play offline.

PARCEL SORT
Send parcels from three queues in the requested order. Give unmatched parcels a
cozy waiting spot, then watch them deliver when their turn arrives.

BLOCK EXIT
Slide smiling blocks toward matching gates. Plan around other pals and help
every block find its way home.

YOUR LITTLE JOURNEY
Explore 300 puzzles across ten themed chapters. Every level is open. Collect
20 stickers, earn local achievement badges, and choose a sticker as your avatar.

A DAILY LITTLE JOY
Try one date-selected puzzle per game each day. Replay to improve your personal
best. Daily milestones celebrate different days without requiring a streak.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Learn with guided practice, ask for free hints, undo a move, or restart with
confirmation. Choose reduced motion, numbered matching symbols, quiet sound
effects, optional vibration, and optional reference move goals.

KEEP YOUR PROGRESS
Unfinished puzzles resume with their moves, timer and undo history. Up to eight
named players can keep separate progress and compare rankings on one device.
Export a backup to a location you choose and preview it before restoring.

Offline play. No ads, purchases, accounts, or automatic analytics. Optional
playtest notes stay on your device until you choose to export them. Rankings are
local, and daily puzzles use your device's date.
